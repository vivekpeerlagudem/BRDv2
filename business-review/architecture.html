8 · Security & Governance
Cross-cutting, applies to every layer
  • Role-based & record-level authorization (no cross-user access)
  • Append-only audit log of every change
  • Immutable sent proposal versions (DB-enforced)
  • Secure buyer links (token in URL fragment, stored hashed)
  • Human approval required for all external actions
  • AI usage governance & per-feature controls
  • Secrets never exposed to frontend or logs
  • Production refuses to start on simulated providers
